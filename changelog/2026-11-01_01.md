## Why Care?

`event-hog-mcp/context-v/` holds the reasoning behind event-hog: the three-layer decision, the CRM spec, and the open issue about the registration desk. It is now an Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2 bundle, so any OKF reader can list what's here and what each doc is for without opening a file.

## What's New?

- **Every doc declares its `type`**, the Train-Case name of its folder: `Decisions`, `Issues`, `Specs`. The decision already had one; the issue and the spec gained theirs.
- **`context-v/index.md`** marks the bundle with `okf_version: "0.2"` and lists its three folders.
- **Three folder indexes**, one line per doc with its lede.

## Under the Hood

- Part of the tree-wide OKF sweep; see the Context Vigilance Kit's plan, [[Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter]].
- Every edit was made by hand. The doc diff is two added `type:` lines and nothing removed. No dates moved: this is a compliance change, not a content change.
- The splash reads `context-v/` into pages, so its loader now skips `index.md`: the indexes are navigation for agents, not pages. The splash builds clean with the same pages as before.
- Before the sweep, `development` and `master` were created on GitHub at `main`'s commit, so the repo has all three branch tiers.
