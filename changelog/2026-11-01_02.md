## Why Care?

## What's New?

- **The three-layer decision is `Decided`**, not `Signed-Off`. `Signed-Off` belongs to specs and plans waiting to be built; a recorded decision is `Decided`. Its `decided_by` and `date_decided` (2026-10-25) stay as written, and the code it chose is in the repo from that day: the `agnostic-crm` D1 schema in `migrations/0001_agnostic_crm.sql`.
- **The registration-desk issue stays `Open`.** The Worker is deployed and the migrations applied, but two of its "Done when" items are unchecked: which deployment the public desk points at, and whether an existing `PEOPLE` key still works.
- **The Pick-a-CRM spec stays `Draft`.** None of what it asks for is in the code yet: no `crm_sync`, no `crm` setting, no CRM adapter. Its open questions are still open.
