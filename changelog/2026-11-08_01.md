## Why Care?

Anyone who copied the prompt on the landing page became a member with full access to every event, and `query_crm` could read the `members` table, connector keys included. A teammate's `PEOPLE` key landed there too once they saved a profile, so any member could act as anyone. Taking someone out of `PEOPLE` also didn't lock them out if they had saved a profile.

## What's New?

- **Invite-only by default.** A landing-page key joins only when `SELF_SERVE` is `"open"`. Otherwise it gets a 401 saying the server is invite-only, and access comes from `PEOPLE` or an admin's `add_person`. Members who already joined keep working.
- **`query_crm` refuses `members` and `connector_people`.**
- **A saved profile never grants access by itself.** Removing someone from `PEOPLE`, or `remove_person`, locks them out.
- **`list_people` shows everyone who can get in**, including connectors that joined from the landing page, and says whether joining is open. `remove_person` can revoke those by email.
- **Edviro's fork deploys on push to `main`**, applying D1 migrations first.

## Under the Hood

- Brings upstream `main` (self-serve onboarding, enrichment, next-step suggestions) into Edviro's fork together with the team tools from its PR #1. PR #1's migration moves from `0006` to `0008`, since upstream's `0006_members` and `0007_enrichments` are already applied.
- `0009` adds `members.self_serve` and marks existing rows whose key is in the landing page's format (48 lowercase hex characters).
- `pages.yml` now runs only upstream, so the fork doesn't publish its own copy of eventhog.cc.
